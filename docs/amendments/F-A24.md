# F-A24

Desks, phase D1 of §10.8.
A desk is a record `{slot, name, members}`: slot 1-8, and each member's rectangle (`[x, y, w, h]`, or null until it is first shown).
Pinned desks are in the board (`desks`); scratch desks, the current and previous desk and a camera per desk are in F-A22's view state.
On a desk, the Board's folder, rectangle and order of every node wait in a snapshot; members stand at their rectangles (a folder carries what it holds, a member whose folder is not on the desk stands loose) and every other item is hidden as a deck's cards are (`.stashed`: hit tests, layout, neighbours, fit and selection skip it), with its links, the Board's loose ink and the shapes outside the desk's folders (a shape goes with the member folder it lay in on the Board, as a folder drag finds it; a Board arrow shows only with both ends on the desk).
An area selection skips hidden ink and shapes.
A save writes the Board from the snapshot whichever desk is shown; an item made on the desk goes to the Board with its folder, the card it was spawned from, or the nearest member.
Leaving a desk records its rectangles and restores the Board exactly; folder changes made on a desk to items that were on the Board are not kept.
A new item joins the desk it is made on; a spawned card joins its parent's desks beside the parent.
Desks and perspectives exclude each other: a perspective goes to the Board first, and a desk to the manual perspective.
Revealing or focusing a hidden item goes to the Board.
A clean tab on a desk that takes another tab's board leaves the desk, takes it, and stands the desk up again (a pinned desk as the board now has it).
Leader and `Alt-Shift` `0`-`9` go to desks (9 is kept for D2's attention desk), leader `` ` `` to the previous desk, `m`/`M` send the selection (a move from a desk, a copy from the Board; `M` follows), and `d` opens new, rename, pin, take off, also on, which desks, delete.
The keys are rows of `web/keys.ts` (F-A23): `desk.N` in the global map (`M-)` … `M-(`, which pages and text fields leave to the board) and the leader, and the leader prefixes `leader-m`, `leader-M`, `leader-d` and `leader-da`.
