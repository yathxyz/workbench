# F-A26

Touch and the camera.
Two fingers move the camera wherever they land, over cards too: the pinch zooms about its midpoint and both fingers pan; while they are down no card scrolls.
The second finger takes the camera only when the first holds nothing (a finger that drags a card, a handle or ink keeps it).
While a finger is on the canvas, framed cards (web, artifact) take no touches, so a second finger on one still counts; a first finger on a frame stays the frame's.
One finger keeps its meaning: bare canvas pans, a card scrolls or drags its own content.
A one-finger pan lifted while moving glides on with its last 100 ms speed, decaying with a 325 ms time constant; a finger that rests 50 ms before the lift does not glide.
A camera flight follows van Wijk and Nuij's smooth zoom path (the zoom changes at an even rate; a long jump pulls back and comes in again) in 380 ms (320 ms since F-A36), and stops where it is at a finger down, a pan or a wheel, running its done step.
`prefers-reduced-motion` is read live: no flight time, no glide, no settle.
