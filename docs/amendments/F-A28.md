# F-A28

Desks, phase D2 of §10.8.
**Tile**: a desk's `layout: 'tile'` (leader `d t`, again frees it) puts its top-level items in rows of cells over the screen at zoom 1 (88 % × 86 % of the fit area), the column count whose cells come nearest 3:2, the last row stretched, in reading order from the items' top-left; it retiles when the members change or the window resizes.
**Promote** (leader `d b`): the Board's place of the selection, and of what a folder holds, becomes its place on the desk; it leaves its Board folder unless that folder is on the desk, and leaving the desk relays the folders it left and moves the Board's other items off it.
**Sticky** (leader `d s`, cards only; `sticky` in the board): the card shows on every desk at its Board place and is in no desk's members; moving it on a desk moves it on the Board.
**Open folder as a desk** (leader `d o`): a scratch desk holding just the folder, reused afterwards.
**Attention desk** (slot 9): the cards Attention lists as `bad`, tiled, built when entered and kept in no record; new ones join while it is shown, except cards kept in the hidden scratch overlay; it cannot be named, pinned, deleted or sent to, and sending from it copies.
The pager shows `9 !` while a card needs a look, and a badge on each desk counts its cards that do.
**Leader `!`** (F-A15's key): the next `bad` card after the active one, on the shown desk if it is there, else on the first desk that holds it, else on the Board; items that are not cards stay in the Attention list.
**Overview** (leader `g`): every desk (the Board, 1-8, 9 while it has cards) drawn far-zoomed, folders as frames and cards as named boxes; arrows move, letters search card names (matching boxes marked, the selection moves to a desk with a match), Backspace takes a letter back, a digit goes to that desk, Enter goes to the selected desk and, with a search, to its first match; Esc stays.
**Recent places** (leader `w`): desks, focus tabs and views, most recent first (12, per device in F-A22's view state); it opens on the previous place; `w`, Tab, ↓, → step on, `W`, Shift-Tab, ↑, ← back, Enter or Space goes, a digit goes to that entry.
**Scratch overlay** (leader `x`; leader `m x` puts the selected cards in, again takes them out; per device): its cards are hidden on every desk and the Board; shown, they are laid out in cells over the middle 72 % of the view at reading size (zoom 1 if the view was below 0.75) and raised; hidden, a desk switch, a perspective or another device's board puts them back, and a save writes their places from before.
Perspectives stay as they are: making them smart desks moves to D3
