# F-A29

Desks, phase D3 of §10.8.
**Perspectives as places**: a perspective (leader `v`) is a place beside the Board, `persp:NAME` in the view state.
The Board (desk 0) always shows the manual layout: leader `0` from a perspective goes back to it, leader `` ` `` returns to the perspective, the pager shows it as `v NAME` (also while it is the place left last), the ring lists it, and it keeps its own camera.
The engine still builds the derived folders in the page, but only while that place is shown: leaving it restores every item exactly (`perspClear`), and a save writes the Board.
**Columns**: leader `d t` now steps a desk through free, `tile` and `columns`.
On a columns desk the top-level items stand side by side in one row, left to right as they stood, at 86 % of the view's height at zoom 1 and their own width (a third of 88 % of the view up to all of it); selecting a card scrolls along the row just far enough to show its column (after the pointer is up, so a drag never slides); a drag or a width change lays the row out again in the new order.
**Revert**: pinning a desk saves its state (`saved`: members, places, layout) in its record; leader `d u` returns the desk to it, leader `d w` makes the desk as it is now the saved state; unpinning drops it.
**One desk per window**: a window keeps its desk and previous desk in `sessionStorage` (`wb-desk-<host>`) over the device's; windows of one host tell each other their desk on the `BroadcastChannel` `wb-desks-<host>`.
Going to a desk another window shows swaps the two; when two windows come up on one desk, the one opened later goes to the Board; a send to a desk another window shows goes to that window; the scratch desks follow the window that changed them, each window keeping the one it shows.
The Board and the perspectives may be in any number of windows; the pager underlines desks shown in another window.
**Agents and `wb`**: the board tools `list_desks`, `go_to_desk` and `send_to_desk` (items are node or folder paths, default the selection; `keep`, `follow`), and `wb desk ls`, `wb desk go N`, `wb desk send N [PATH]... [--keep] [--follow]` over web.sock (§3.7.4)
