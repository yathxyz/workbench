# F-A31

**A pen's barrel button pans and never inks.**
A pen pointer whose `button` is 1 or 2, or whose `buttons` hold 2 or 4 (the barrel as middle, the way X11 Wacom drivers send it, or as right, the way Windows Ink does), takes the camera like a middle-button drag, hovering or touching, over the canvas and over cards, with every tool; the eraser end (`buttons` 32) keeps erasing.
A pen stroke or erase that gains a barrel button becomes a pan, and the stroke's ink is dropped.
A tip that lands within 250 ms and 24 px of where a pen pan ended carries the pan on, so a driver that reports barrel and tip as two contacts leaves no dot.
No context menu opens within 600 ms of a pen pan.
A pointer that a middle or Alt pan holds starts no ink or shape (an Alt-drag with the pen tool left a dot before) (N62)
