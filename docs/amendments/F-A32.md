# F-A32

**Images and the clipboard on the canvas.**
An `image` node is `{kind:"image", name, blob, mime, iw, ih}`: `blob` is the sha256 of the bytes that `POST /api/blob` stored, shown from `/blob/<hash>`; in demo mode the node keeps a `data:image/…;base64` URL in `src` instead (at most 2 MB).
Only those two forms are ever rendered or opened.
PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP and AVIF are sent as they are; any other image the browser can draw (SVG, BMP, TIFF on Safari) is redrawn as a PNG first.
A new image card shows the whole picture within 640 × 480, centred on where the image arrived; its header has ↗ (open in a tab), ⧉ (the image on the system clipboard as PNG) and 1:1 (natural size); with the select tool its body drags it like the header, and the pen draws on it as on any card.
At far zoom the picture stays, under a strip with its name, in place of the snapshot text.
**Ways in:** a paste on the board, image files dropped on the canvas (PDFs make pdf cards, F-A40; any other dropped file is refused with a toast, so the browser never replaces the board with it), and the palette's "Paste onto the canvas" (the async clipboard, for touch screens and Safari, which fires no paste on a page without a text field) and "New image from a file…".
**Keys:** `C-c`, `C-x`, `C-v` (and `s-` on a Mac) are canvas keymap rows (§6.4) whose commands return false, so the browser keeps the key and fires its own `copy`, `cut` and `paste` events, which the board handles only while the keyboard is on the board: a shell, a page, a text field, an Edwin frame or a framed card keeps its own clipboard.
**Paste** takes, in order: items copied on a board (`application/x-workbench-items`), image files, then text: one http(s) URL is a web card, one line of at most 120 characters a text label, anything longer a page holding the text (named after its first line).
The pasted items land centred under the pointer while it is over the canvas, else at the middle of the view, and are picked.
**Copy** takes the picked cards, ink and shapes (with nothing picked, the selected card): image, sketch, web, ref and page cards are duplicated (a page as a new page with the same text), with their own ink, the arrows and edges among them (an arrow bound at both ends to copied cards comes along even when it is not selected; a pasted shape is in no folder); shells, agents, artifacts and folders are not copied and the toast says so.
Text already selected on the page copies as text.
The `text/plain` side holds the URL, the page text, the label or the card name.
A copy of one image card with nothing drawn on it (and its ⧉) also writes the image to the system clipboard as PNG with its URL as text (async clipboard, where the browser allows it), so it pastes into another program.
Each copy is also remembered on the device (`localStorage` `wb-clip-<host>`, try/catch) under its text side: a paste whose text matches it, from a clipboard that carries no board items (the async clipboard of the palette's paste, or the system clipboard after an image copy), pastes the remembered items, so an image comes back as a card on the same bytes; a text that matches nothing pastes as text.
**Cut** copies, then removes exactly what was copied.
