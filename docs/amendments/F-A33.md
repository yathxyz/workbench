# F-A33

Key hygiene.
A held leader is one press: its auto-repeat is swallowed by the board's key dispatcher and not forwarded by an artifact's bridge, so holding Ctrl-Space neither passes the key through (F-A23's leader twice) nor reopens the leader.
Space held to pan (`pan.hold`) is let go on a window `blur` and when the page is hidden (`visibilitychange`) as well as on its keyup, which never arrives when the key comes up in another window or inside a frame
