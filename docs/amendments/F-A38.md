# F-A38

Query desks (§10.8, the Model's smart desks): a desk 1-8 with a `query` holds the cards and folders it matches.
Words all match; `-word` negates; commas give alternatives inside a word.
`tag:T` (`tagsOf`, the automatic tags too), `kind:K` (page or note, shell or terminal, agent, folder, artifact, web, sketch, image, repl, ref), `host:H` (`@` optional; `local` is the serving daemon), `board:/PATH` (the item or anything under it), `state:S` (`bad` is what needs a look; else a session state such as `running`, `waiting_permission`, `done`), `name:TEXT`, and a bare word for `name:`.
A card inside a matching folder goes with the folder; addresses, ghosts, derived folders, sticky cards and the scratch overlay's cards are not matched.
As desk 9, its members are found again each time it is entered (keeping the place a card had on it), a card that starts to match joins it while it is shown (on the 20 s status tick), and one that stops matching stays until the desk is left; nothing is sent to it, nothing is taken off it, and sending from it copies.
Leader `d q` makes one on a free number (tiled, named by its query, scratch until pinned) or changes the shown one's query; an empty query makes it a plain desk.
It can be renamed, pinned (the record keeps `query`), laid out and deleted.
The agent tool `query_desk {query, desk?}` and `wb desk query [N] WORD...` make one; `list_desks` shows the query.
It reads the board only: a query over tasks or notes belongs to brain (brain `docs/API.md`), and Workbench has no brain client yet
