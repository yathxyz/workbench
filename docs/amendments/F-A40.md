# F-A40

**PDFs on the canvas.**
A `pdf` node is `{kind:"pdf", name, path?, blob?, src?, pos?:{page, y}, pages?}`: `path` a PDF on the host read through `GET /api/raw` (ranges), `blob` the sha256 of PDF bytes in the blob store (a file dropped, pasted or opened from the device, at most 32 MB), `src` a `data:application/pdf;base64` URL in demo mode (at most 2 MB).
pdf.js 6.4.299, its legacy build (`vendor/pdfjs/`, pinned by `nix/web.nix`, outside the npm lock; no eval path, XFA off) loads with the first pdf card and draws in the page itself, not in a frame.
**Pages** are boxes at the card's width, sized from the first page until each is read; the ones in view and half a card around get a bitmap at the camera's zoom × device pixel ratio (at most 4096 px a side), drawn again when the zoom settles; at most 6 bitmaps per card and 18 per tab stay, least recently seen first, none at far zoom, off the desk or in a deck.
A text layer over each drawn page takes the selection; link annotations become link boxes.
**Quote** (❝, `q`): the selection becomes a `ref` card whose `src` is `{node, page, prefix, suffix}` (32 characters each side, W3C TextQuoteSelector), with a ref edge; its ↗ finds the text again, on its page first and then on any page (a changed file), scrolls there and lights it up, or says the quote is gone and shows the page.
**Peek:** a link to a place in the file draws that place (280 units from the destination) into an `image` card beside the PDF with `at:{node, page, y}` and a ref edge; ⤴ on it scrolls the PDF there; an existing peek of the same place is jumped to instead.
Links to http(s) URLs open in a new tab; other links do nothing.
**Reading position** `pos` is saved in the card (debounced 700 ms), so every device opens the PDF where it was left; the badge says `p. N / M`.
**Keys** (§6.4, interior `pdf`, map `pdf`, while the pages scroller has the keyboard): `j`/`k` and arrows scroll, `SPC`/`S-SPC`/PgDn/PgUp a screen, `n`/`p` a page, `g`/`G`/Home/End the ends, `q` quote, `/` find (again: the next match), `#` go to page; every other key is the board's.
⇢ sends the selection along pipe edges (into a media card's prompt, F-A41); ⌕ finds; ↗ opens the bytes in a tab (a download).
**Ways in:** a `.pdf` ghost of the file tree opens as a pdf card on the host; PDF files dropped or pasted on the canvas; the palette's "Open a PDF from this device…" and "Open a PDF on the host…".
**Agents:** `read_pdf {path, from?, to?}` returns pages as text with `--- name p. N ---` markers (default the reading page and nine after it, at most 60000 characters), `open_pdf {file}` opens a host PDF and answers its card path; `read_node` on a pdf card returns the reading page and the next.
Annotations (ink in page coordinates, a sidecar page) and fetching a URL are not part of this.
