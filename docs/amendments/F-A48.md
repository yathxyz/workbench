# F-A48

**Agents is a place to work** (2026-11-17).
The Agents main view is available without the mesh API: it lists the hub's agent sessions from `SESS`, adding observed sessions from `OBS` when configured, and never requires adopting a canvas card.
A hub shell appears only through an observed session running in it (F-A47).
When the Agents view has at least 700 px of available width, list and detail show together; below 700 px of view width, including when the dock narrows it, it shows one pane with a Sessions back action (F-A13), without a divider.
**Layout.**
A focusable vertical separator between list and detail takes mouse or touch pointer drags, clamped to a list of at least 200 px and detail of at least 360 px.
Left/Right move it 16 px; double-click resets to the default (34%, at least 260 px, subject to those clamps).
Its width is saved as `meshW` with the board's UI state, like the docked terminal's `paneW`.
Mesh activity and view resizes do not reapply the saved width while a divider drag is in progress.
With a chat or terminal mounted, the header shows the title and a single metadata line: harness · model · host · folder · mode · state, omitting unknown values.
The folder loses its left side when shortened and keeps its full value in `title`.
Header actions wrap within the pane down to 320 px; Kill session retains distinct styling and confirmation.
Prompt, reply, start/activity times, counts, reasoning and the other facts live behind Details, closed by default.
Its open state lasts for this page only, including across selections.
The disclosure overlays the session in a bounded, independently scrolling panel, never consuming the session's layout space or moving the actions.
Without a mounted session, all facts remain open as the content.
The mounted session fills the remaining height (`min-height: 0` throughout its flex chain), keeps at least half the pane at 1280×800 and 900×700 even with a 2,000-character prompt, and leaves a bottom composer gap consistent with an agent card.
A terminal refits on divider and disclosure changes.
**Sources.**
A reachable hub PTY takes precedence over an observed attach and opens without spawning.
A hub ACP session opens its journal (see **ACP transcript reader** below), composer, permission and elicitation controls through `AcpBackend`; it runs on piped stdio and has no terminal of its own.
When that row also has an eligible observed attach, Transcript is the default and a button or `t` from the list switches between Transcript and Terminal.
The ACP viewer stays mounted while hidden, preserving the reader's scroll position, drafts, any Edwin prompt editor, and request forms.
Hub viewers are transient and not board nodes; changing row, hub sid, kind or hub incarnation (`createdAt`) disposes the viewer without killing the session.
Card presence, liveness and attach changes update the detail in place.
**Observed terminal.**
An observed attach is eligible only while the observed record is live, has a validated `attach`, and `/api/info.attach` is true.
For Claude Code (`harness:"claude"` or `attach.kind:"claude"`), a committed selection opens it immediately unless the row has a hub source.
A click, phone tap or Enter on the highlighted row commits the selection; the initial highlight and Up/Down or j/k navigation through visible rows do not spawn.
When both panes are visible, clicking a row leaves keyboard focus in the list; Enter on the highlight or a click inside the terminal focuses it.
In the single-pane layout, selecting a row that opens an attach terminal focuses it because the list is hidden.
Other eligible observed-only rows keep the explicit "Open terminal" action.
An unavailable observed terminal shows the observed summary, a reason and setup guidance; if eligibility is lost while the terminal is selected it closes without remounting the detail shell.
A joined row can return to Transcript from that summary.
If eligibility returns while Terminal or the committed observed-only selection remains active, a fresh viewer opens.
A failed attach shows its reason and an explicit Retry with a new sid, never a shell-start prompt.
Each launch spawns a unique per-mount `attach_<observed id>_<random>` PTY with `attach`, not browser `cmd`.
Two pages never share it.
It is killed and forgotten on selection change, switching back to Transcript, view leave, Sessions back or unload, including a kill requested before open completes, and must not kill the observed agent.
Only PTYs with that prefix are excluded from Attention's orphan list; they are not Agents rows.
Each remains a real PTY in `/api/sessions` until forgotten.
Escape returns to the list without changing the highlight; text fields, terminals and Edwin retain their own keys (§6.4).
With Edwin ready, the ACP composer can open a local unsaved buffer in an Edwin frame (F-A27, F-A39); `C-c C-c` sends its current text as the normal prompt, and `C-c C-k` cancels editing and restores the text to the composer unsent.
Rows group by repository and nest by parent as F-A47 specifies; harness, model, worktree and branch are truncated as needed but retain full title attributes.
Detail shows repository, worktree, branch and a selectable known parent ("started by"), plus harness, model, host, cwd, mode, thinking/reasoning, start and activity times only when known; ACP model and thinking come from journaled session/config-option updates, not invented values or a hub protocol change.
**Ownership.**
A card made by `adoptOrphan` carries `adopted:true`, saved with its board node.
Closing a marked card only removes the card; closing any unmarked card kills its session, including after a reload.
Reset board applies the same rule per card and kills the docked terminal.
Only confirmation of a newly created replacement session (ACP `status:"new"` or PTY `new:true`) clears the mark and saves that ownership change; a spawn request that attaches to an existing session leaves it borrowed.
Agents selection never claims ownership.
A separate explicit "Kill session" action requires confirmation before ending a hub session, regardless of ownership.

**ACP transcript reader (F-A48).**
In Agents only, when the ACP prompt editor's readiness gate is open, the journal is one Edwin frame at the detail's width, including on a phone.
Its session-named `wb-agent-transcript-mode` buffer is local, unfiled, unreplicated, read-only in all editing states and killed with the viewer.
The mode has no Org, Markdown, note or link actions.
The composer, existing Edwin prompt editor, permission and elicitation panels, plan, mode picker, Stop and detail metadata do not move into the reader.
Canvas ACP cards keep their DOM journals.

- **Text.**
  Uncoloured and line-oriented: `Prompt: <first line>` heads a turn; `Assistant:` and `Thinking:` head message bodies.
  `Tool: <kind> [<status>] <title>` heads a tool, with `Location:`, `Diff:` and `Output:` below as present.
  Continuations and bodies are indented two spaces.
  Journal fields normalize CRLF to LF, including across streamed chunks; aside from LF and tab, C0 controls and DEL display as control pictures, and U+2028 and U+2029 display as literal `\u2028` and `\u2029`.
  Tool output and diffs use the DOM journal's content, placeholders and omitted-context lines, except an oversized diff shows only the summary, never the expandable new file.
  Raw inputs shown only in a permission panel stay there.
  `· <status>` lines carry lifecycle events and request resolutions, a blank line marks a normal turn end, and outstanding requests show `· Permission: waiting` or `· Elicitation: waiting` at the tail until the journal resolves them.
  No form answers are written into the buffer.
- **Updates.**
  The journal reducer and replay cursor are shared with the DOM renderer.
  The reader paints at most 64 dirty items per animation frame, batching new items in one splice and retaining positions outside Edwin.
  A chunk appends within its message; a tool update replaces its changed text in place.
  Point, mark and window start track the old text across edits; point at EOF or on the final empty tail line (where Edwin's `G` lands) follows new text.
  Moving away stops following until point returns there.
  Updates never take focus or accumulate undo.
- **Fallback.**
  Without a ready Edwin, the DOM journal is the only presentation.
  It can promote on the first ready transition only if nothing in the ACP viewer is focused, no prompt editor is open and no form elicitation is pending; otherwise it stays DOM for that mount.
  Focus in the attach terminal does not block promotion.
  A frame failure, killed reader buffer or switch to another buffer restores the DOM journal for the rest of that mount.
  Loss of the edwind connection leaves the local reader in place.
