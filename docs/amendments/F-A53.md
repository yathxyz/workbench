# F-A53 — Navigate and fold an agent transcript

The Agents detail reader extends F-A48 with a heading per submitted turn, labelled user and assistant messages, and thoughts folded by default.
Each tool has one heading with kind, status and title; its locations, input, diff and output are initially folded beneath it.
Plan progress, the active step and current settings share a compact status block, and permission and elicitation headings carry the original request sequence and change in place to show their outcome.
All headings and fold boundaries come from journal records, never from markup, paths, URLs or other content supplied by an agent.
The reader builds replay in bounded paints, appends streamed text at its message, and updates a tool in place without rebuilding unrelated text or stealing focus.
Point, mark and the viewport track the text the reader chose; point at the tail follows new text, movement away suspends following, and an explicit follow command resumes it.
Within the transcript, `C-c n` and `C-c p` visit the next and previous turn, `C-c ]` and `C-c [` visit the next and previous tool, `C-c TAB` toggles the section at point, `C-c a` folds or unfolds all sections, and `C-c f` follows the tail.
In normal and motion state, `za` and Tab toggle the item at point, `zc` closes it, `zo` and `zO` open it, and `zM` and `zR` close and open all items respectively.
The phone reader offers the same actions without requiring a keyboard.
A collapsed turn contains its tools and thoughts; reopening it preserves their individual choices, and a tool jump opens the enclosing turn and lands on the tool's visible header without changing the tool's fold.
Fold choices belong to item identities in one journal incarnation and survive live updates, same-incarnation replay and closing and reopening the detail pane, not replacement of the session.
Edwin's closed ranges are read back after editor commands and restored in the same render callback as each journal edit, so an append never briefly shows hidden text or undoes the reader's last fold command.
Folds require Edwin's public owner-supplied buffer fold API; with an older Edwin the reader retains navigation and follow but does not advertise fold commands.
Plain `j`, `k`, Enter and Escape stay with Edwin, leader Escape returns to the list, and the DOM composer and live request panels remain the editing and answering surfaces.
