# N51

Two browser contexts (two devices).
A card shown in a focus tab is saved at its canvas rectangle.
A workspace and a focus tab entered in A are not in the board file and not in B, and are back in A after a reload.
With A in the tag perspective the board file holds A's manual layout exactly, with no derived node and no view key.
B moves a card: A takes it without a bar and stays in its perspective, and B never leaves manual (F-A22).
Deleting a card locally or on another device closes its focus tab, including the saved tab list.
Deleting the shown tab restores the canvas camera and wheel panning; deleting a background tab leaves the current one shown.
