# N62

A pen's barrel (F-A31; demo page, CDP pen events).
With the select, pen and erase tools: the barrel pressed in hover as middle and as right, dragged 200 px over bare canvas and over a page, pans 200 px with no new stroke and no context menu; the tip landing while the barrel is held, a right barrel pressed 24 px into a stroke, and a tip that lands 60 ms after a barrel pan where it ended all pan and leave no stroke.
A plain pen stroke and a pen tap well after a pan each add one stroke and do not move the camera
