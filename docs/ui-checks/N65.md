# N65

(demo) A right-click on a card's name opens the palette at the pointer with Delete, Tag and Arrange and selects the card; on a folder's name, Free, Workspace and Delete the folder without the hidden reconnect and list buttons; on the canvas, New page/shell/folder here and Fit everything; inside a free folder, New page here makes a page in that folder with its top-left at the pointer.
Shift+right-click on the canvas and a right-click in a text field are not prevented and open no palette.
Delete from the menu removes the card on the second pick, not the first (F-A34)
